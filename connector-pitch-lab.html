Why this structure (one paragraph of doctrine)

Cold email to strangers scored 0/149. The connector model joins a flow that already exists: educators and coaches who already hold the audience our buyer sits in. The video arm converts attention → code claims; the email arms test which motive (money vs. capacity) moves a partner; the stats page keeps all of it falsifiable in public. If any single arm works, the other two inherit its copy. If none do, the ledger will say so before we do.
