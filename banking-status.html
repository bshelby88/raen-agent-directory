For humans

On November 16, 2026, customers of Chase, Wells Fargo, Bank of America and U.S. Bank spent an afternoon unable to transact — roughly 4,800 Downdetector reports for Chase at the 2 p.m. ET peak, confirmed by the banks as a software bug, resolved by evening. The same year, six FDIC-insured banks failed, led by Nano Banc's $736M collapse. Humans found out about the outage the way humans find out about everything: refreshing Downdetector and the news.

Machines have no such reflex. An agent treasury running payroll, an indexer scheduling spend, a payment bot routing around a failing rail — none of them can "notice" that Chase is down. They either hard-code an assumption that banking always works, or they guess. Today there is no paid, machine-readable, per-bank outage signal. Scraping Downdetector is fragile and free signals carry no SLA and no evidence trail.

The Banking Status Feed is the product shaped like that gap: a per-bank status check, machine-readable JSON, priced at a fraction of a cent so an agent can check before every large transfer, not just after a headline. Like the Compliance Feed, it is evidence-first: every status change carries a link to the source (bank statements, wire notices, FDIC filings, corroborated outage reports) and a checked-at timestamp. The machines that already settle on-chain don't need banking — but they need to know who else does.

Why RAEN? Because we have skin in this exact claim: on Nov 16, while the outage cascaded, our own settles cleared on-chain. USDC settles when banks close. Selling the mirror-image signal — when don't they? — is the honest sibling of that receipt.

Why CONCEPT and not live

The fleet's law is demand validation before infrastructure — the rule that came out of the graveyard edition of this ledger. The Compliance Feed was built against a paying crawler's evidenced need; the Banking Status Feed has only a thesis and one very good case study. So: concept page now, machine-readable seed now, endpoint when the demand gate allows — with the build decision published on the Daily Ledger either way.

TRUTH POLICY: this page describes a plan, and says so in every register — the HTML badge, the meta description, and the JSON status field. No prices are live, no endpoints exist, no verdicts are issued. The one factual claim here is fully sourced: banks had outages today and six banks failed this year; RAEN settled through today's outage window. Kill-test culture applies: if this concept never earns a build, the ledger records that too.

Payment IS auth. Two-POST flow: (1) POST without payment → HTTP 402 challenge (EIP-3005, EIP-3009 transferWithAuthorization); (2) re-send the same POST with the signed quote in the PAYMENT-SIGNATURE header → 200 + result. No API keys, no accounts, per-call USDC settlement on Base. Manifest prices below fetched 2026-11-16.
