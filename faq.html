1. How do I pay a RAEN endpoint?

RAEN sells per-call API access over the x402 protocol, exact-scheme USDC on Base mainnet (eip155:8453). The flow:

1. POST the route with no payment header  →  HTTP 402 + payment challenge
2. Your client signs the challenge (USDC on Base)
3. Retry with the X-PAYMENT header         →  HTTP 200 + your result

Every host publishes /pricing.md and a live x402 manifest. The 402 challenge is authoritative on price — if the configured price ever changes, the challenge reflects it first. All payments settle to the canonical treasury 0x9b8a27e03ceba490953cda8ab1291c0e13baf72f.

Verified 2026-11-15: /pricing.md returns 200 on the fleet hosts (e.g. sentry-forge).

4. Why did my payment fail on dispute-forge?

Known problem, not your fault. Dispute-forge has an elevated failure rate — as of 2026-11-15, 242 of 464 all-time challenges failed (54 of 56 in the last 24h), and it is under active investigation. The retry that has worked historically: re-issue a fresh challenge and sign it fresh — don't replay a stale signature.

Never blame the buyer's client first: if it keeps failing, reply here or open an issue — the report goes to engineering the same day with repro steps. Live failure counts: /stats → dispute-forge.
