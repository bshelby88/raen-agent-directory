1. How do I pay a RAEN endpoint?

RAEN sells per-call API access over the x402 protocol, exact-scheme USDC on Base mainnet (eip155:8453). The flow:

1. POST the route with no payment header  →  HTTP 402 + payment challenge
2. Your client signs the challenge (USDC on Base)
3. Retry with the X-PAYMENT header         →  HTTP 200 + your result

Every host publishes /pricing.md and a live x402 manifest. The 402 challenge is authoritative on price — if the configured price ever changes, the challenge reflects it first. All payments settle to the canonical treasury 0x9b8a27e03ceba490953cda8ab1291c0e13baf72f.

Verified 2026-11-15: /pricing.md returns 200 on the fleet hosts (e.g. sentry-forge).

2. Is RAEN legit — have people actually paid?

3. Does the $5 Sentry Forge pack work with CFPB complaints?

Yes. POST /api/dispute-pack ($5.00 USDC per request) returns 8 markdown files: collector letter, original-creditor letter, two CFPB complaint drafts (collector + creditor), bureau dispute outlines, court-records search guide, action checklist, and evidence inventory.

See for yourself free: /api/dispute-pack/example returns the same 8-file structure with synthetic, anonymized content — also at /sample.

Verified 2026-11-15 against the live example response. Information and document preparation only — not legal advice, no outcome guarantee.

4. Why did my payment fail on dispute-forge?

Known problem, not your fault. Dispute-forge has an elevated failure rate — as of 2026-11-15, 242 of 464 all-time challenges failed (54 of 56 in the last 24h), and it is under active investigation. The retry that has worked historically: re-issue a fresh challenge and sign it fresh — don't replay a stale signature.

Never blame the buyer's client first: if it keeps failing, reply here or open an issue — the report goes to engineering the same day with repro steps. Live failure counts: /stats → dispute-forge.

5. Do you offer subscriptions or bulk pricing?

Truthful answer: the API surfaces are per-call only today. One subscription-shaped product is live on the storefront: Contract Watch — $19 per 30 days, weekly contract re-reviews, recurring. Pack pricing for steady-volume API buyers is on the roadmap queue, not live — we won't sell it before it exists. If your volume is steady, tell us what you'd need: real buyer volume is what moves pack pricing up the queue.

7. Can I get a free trial?

No zero-value challenges exist, by protocol design — x402 facilitators reject $0 payment requirements. We won't pretend otherwise. What exists instead — free discovery surfaces, verified live 2026-11-15:

  • /api/dispute-pack/example and /sample — the full 8-file output structure, synthetic content, no payment
  • /pricing.md on every host — prices and routes before you commit
  • First Call Free — try royal-ruby for net $0 (you pay, then get rebated; one per wallet per 30 days)
  • Storefront free tier — first 10 dispute letters free with email registration (delivered by email, not per-call)
