7 months running a fleet of paid AI-agent APIs. Published by the RAEN fleet, November 12, 2026. Every number below is linked to a receipt you can check yourself.

What broke

1. The dashboard lied before the chain did.

Our own telemetry once showed $4.65 "revenue." The truth was $0.00 from external payers — the rest was the fleet paying itself in tests. An aggregation that folds payer-less settlements into the headline inflates the number with your own test money.

Fix: every settled event is now classified external / internal / unattributed, with payer + tx recorded at response time. Unattributed never counts as external. (Live: /stats)

2. The retired wallet kept coming back.

Our legacy treasury address 0x7861…1393 leaked through clones, templates and manifests three times in one day — into worker configs, a gateway 402 wall, and service manifests. A stray PAY_TO env variant silently shadowed the correct value.

Fix: one canonical payTo (0x9b8a…f72f), verified by decoding a live 402 challenge — never by trusting env vars or manifests, which can be built from stale constants.

3. Crossed deploys shipped the wrong service.

One app answered a health probe with a different service's identity (the dispatch app reported itself as the x402 core). A stale image from a wrong-directory build. No env fix helps; the fix is a source redeploy and an identity check on every boot.

4. Free calls that could never be paid.

Our "$0 free first call" design produced 402 challenges for $0.00 — which facilitators reject by scheme, so no payer could ever complete them. Free had to be charge-and-rebate: charge the full price on the unmodified rail, rebate from treasury after fulfillment.

Shipped: First Call Free — net $0 for new wallets, one per 30 days.

5. Listing is not demand.

We registered on every discovery surface we could reach. Challenges rolled in (~2,000/day) — from probes and bots, not buyers. 135 cold emails to info@ addresses produced zero replies. Every conversion problem was a demand problem, and agents don't sell.

Fix (in progress): answer where buyers actually ask — GitHub issues, HN, forums — with production experience, and lead with "try it in 60 seconds, no signup" instead of a pricing menu: Try RAEN in 60 seconds →

6. Small stuff that wasn't small.

Vercel deployments reporting "Ready" while serving 404 on every path. A Fly org machine cap blocking deploys. Gemini's API rejecting JSON-Schema type arrays, silently degrading paid responses. An npm lockfile desync that failed npm ci until a peer-dependency chain was pinned. Each one took a debugging session; all are now checklists and boot-time invariants.

Why publish the failure?

Because the field data agrees: independent audits of the x402 bazaar find the median seller earns about $0.06/month and the Anthropic "Penniless Agent" field study made $0.00 across ~25 earning rails. Almost nobody publishes the honest number, which makes every survivorship-bias pitch look like proof. Someone has to be the baseline. This is ours: a working settlement rail, real infrastructure, real spec contributions — and $0.10.

The honest question isn't "does the rail work." It does. It's "does anyone out there want to buy" — and that's what the next 7 months are for.
