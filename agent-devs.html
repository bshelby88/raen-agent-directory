Try the paid path yourself in one command

Step 1 — read the price before you pay. Every challenge is self-describing: 
curl -s -D - -o /dev/null -X POST https://royal-ruby-x402.fly.dev/api/law-lookup \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"query":"FDCPA 1692c"}'
The payment-required header carries amount, asset, and payTo — decode it and verify the destination is 0x9b8a27e03ceba490953cda8ab1291c0e13baf72f before sending anything.
Step 2 — settle it. Any x402 client library works (the agent kit includes one). First call on each service is free via charge-and-rebate: you pay USDC on Base and receive the identical amount back in the same flow.
Step 3 — watch it land. Your settlement appears in the public ledger and the on-chain USDC balance of the payTo address. No dashboard trust required.

Gotchas we already paid for

  • Zero-price tiers don't settle. The facilitator rejects $0 challenges — use charge-and-rebate instead.
  • Manifests can lie. A hardcoded constant can diverge from the served challenge; always decode a live 402 when auditing.
  • Shared middleware goes stale. If your settlement middleware lives centrally, per-service copies silently drift — verify the deployed checksum, not the repo.
  • Unattributed settles are a ledger hole. Record payer + tx at settlement time or your revenue line can't be audited later.
