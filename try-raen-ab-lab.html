Try-RAEN A/B copy lab

RAEN's buyer funnel entry (try-raen.html) is running a public A/B copy experiment. This page is the experiment record: hypothesis, variants, measurement, decision rule. Last updated 2026-11-13 (experiment start).

Why this surface

try-raen.html is the funnel entry every buyer journey is pointed at — from the directory index, llms.txt, the cookbook, and the connector pages. Fleet telemetry (2026-11-13): ~2,300 challenges/24h with zero settles, so the leak to fix is attention → demo-run → paid call, not pricing. The first measurable step is the demo-run rate on this page.

Hypothesis

Control (A) sells the mechanism ("try RAEN in 60 seconds — decode a live 402 challenge"). Variant (B) sells the buyer's outcome ("give your agent its first paid API call"). For a developer audience deciding whether the pattern is worth wiring in, outcome framing should lift the demo-click rate. If it doesn't, mechanism framing is the honest winner — the demo is the product here.

Everything below the fold (steps 1–3, curl one-liner, ledger links) is identical for both variants. Only H1, subhead, and CTA label differ. Variant assignment is a 50/50 coin flip, persisted per browser session, and exposed as data-ab-variant on <body>.

Measurement funnel

Per-variant beacons hit the collector's public hit counter:

  • Page load → src=try-raen-a or try-raen-b
  • Demo click → src=try-raen-a-demo or try-raen-b-demo
curl -s https://settlement-collector.fly.dev/hits.json | python -m json.tool

Metrics per variant: page loads, demo clicks, demo-click rate. Down-funnel (challenge→settle) is read from /stats; at current traffic volumes it is directional only. Known limits, stated honestly: session persistence means reloads re-count a load (symmetric across variants, doesn't bias the split); JS-disabled visitors aren't counted at all; and bot traffic inflates loads, not clicks.

Decision rule

  • Sample gate: no verdict before ≥100 page loads per variant.
  • Adopt B if its demo-click rate is ≥ 1.5× A's at the sample gate (small numbers demand a wide margin, not a p-value).
  • Kill B if its rate is ≤ A's at the gate — revert to single-copy control.
  • Iterate if between 1× and 1.5× — new variant C tests a sharper outcome claim.
